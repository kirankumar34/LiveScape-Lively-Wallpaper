# Privacy Policy for LiveScape Pro

**Last Updated:** June 2026

## Overview

LiveScape Pro is a Chrome extension that transforms the browser's new tab page with live wallpapers, customizable widgets, productivity tools, and personalization features.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

---

## Information We Collect

### We Do Not Collect

LiveScape Pro does **not** collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any of the following:

* Personal information
* Names
* Email addresses
* Phone numbers
* Payment information
* Authentication credentials
* Browsing history
* Website activity
* Keystrokes
* Personal communications
* Location data

---

## Information Stored Locally

To provide customization features, LiveScape Pro stores the following data locally on your device using Chrome Storage:

* Wallpaper preferences
* Uploaded wallpapers
* Widget positions
* Widget appearance settings
* Task lists
* Notes
* Bookmarks and quick-access links
* Scheduler configurations
* Theme preferences
* Audio settings
* Performance preferences

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

---

## Weather Information

If weather functionality is enabled, LiveScape Pro may request weather information from third-party weather providers.

Only the minimum information required to retrieve weather data is used. LiveScape Pro does not store or sell weather-related information.

---

## Third-Party Services

LiveScape Pro may interact with third-party services solely for optional features such as:

* Weather information
* Wallpaper content sources

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

---

## Data Sharing

LiveScape Pro does not:

* Sell user data
* Rent user data
* Share user data with advertisers
* Transfer user data to third parties

---

## Data Security

All user preferences and customization data are stored locally within the browser using Chrome's secure storage mechanisms.

No personal information is transmitted to external servers by LiveScape Pro.

---

## Children's Privacy

LiveScape Pro does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
